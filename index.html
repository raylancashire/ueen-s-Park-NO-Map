Classification uses a date-based linear trend, expressed relative to each site's mean concentration: strong improvement ≤ −5%/year; moderate improvement −5% to −2%/year; broadly stable −2% to +2%/year; moderate deterioration +2% to +5%/year; strong deterioration ≥ +5%/year.
